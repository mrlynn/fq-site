    Workdays that know about holidays.

        A nudge before the date, not after.

        Turn on any of four notifications. They're all off until you do.

        • Milestones, on the day or 1, 2, or 5 workdays ahead, set for each one
        • The last two weeks of the quarter, the day the countdown turns amber
        • The first day of a quarter
        • 30 days before the fiscal year ends

        They come at 9:00, or the time you choose, and follow your calendar and holidays. The Mac app plans a month ahead and plans again whenever a milestone, a setting, or the date changes. It opens at login, so the plan stays current. The iPhone app plans each time it opens.

          Widgets for the desktop and the Home Screen.

          Small and medium widgets on the Mac desktop and the iPhone Home Screen, plus three Lock Screen sizes on iPhone. The medium widget sets the quarter, the half, and the year side by side, because they start on different days even when they end together.

          Your customers' years don't start when yours does.

          Add the fiscal calendars of the companies you sell to or work with, by name and the day their year starts. The panel lists each one with its quarter and days left, and turns amber in their last two weeks, so you see their quarter end coming.

          Calendars with a short transition year, or quarters that don't follow whole months, can be imported from a file.

          Make it look like yours.

          • BackgroundThe system's own, a color, one of six gradients, or a photo.
          • TypeTen typefaces, the weight of the big number, and three text sizes.
          • ColorLight, dark, or automatic text, and your own accent.

          Years that end on a Saturday.

          Retailers and many companies keep a 52-week year that ends on the same weekday every year, with a 53rd week every five or six years. Choose Weeks (4-4-5) under Calendar and set when the year ends: the last Saturday of a month, or the Saturday nearest its end.

          Quarters are 13 weeks, and the long year's extra week goes to Q4 or Q1. The 4-4-5, 4-5-4, or 5-4-4 pattern splits each quarter into periods, which the year pictures draw. The FY number can follow the year the fiscal year ends, or the year it starts, as retail calendars name it. One click sets up the National Retail Federation's 4-5-4 calendar.

          A customer's 4-4-5 calendar can be imported from a file, beside your own.

            Ask Siri, or build it into a shortcut.

            Fiscal Quarter answers Siri and Spotlight on the Mac and iPhone with no setup, using your calendar, holidays, and milestones. In the Shortcuts app it adds four actions you can build on:

            • Get Fiscal Quarter, such as FY26-Q4, for yours or a saved calendar
            • Get Time Left in the quarter, half, or year, in days, workdays, weeks, or weekends
            • Get Fiscal Quarter for Date, for any day
            • Get Next Milestone, as a date

            Name a document for the quarter, put the days left in a status message, or set a reminder for the next forecast. The answers here are live for today.
